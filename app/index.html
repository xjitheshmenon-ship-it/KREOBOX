07 — Questions

Short answers.
Not whitepapers.

How is 48-to-72-hour install actually possible? +

Because nothing is fabricated on-site. Every panel is cut, laminated and edge-banded at the factory against the planner's CNC-ready cut-list, then held as standardized SKU inventory at the depot. Installation is flat-pack assembly to a known sequence — closer to IKEA assembly than carpentry.

Is this plywood or chipboard? +

Plywood. India's interior market runs on plywood and laminate, not the chipboard-and-melamine of European modular systems. Kreobox is locally tuned — pre-cut plywood panels, laminate codes from the catalog, OEM-standardized hardware.

What if my room has a weird shape or odd dimensions? +

The planner snaps to your room outline. With 100 standardised panel SKUs combining into 1000+ catalog products, most real-world configurations are covered — irregular dimensions are handled with filler panels and end-caps that the planner generates automatically. For truly bespoke layouts, your project converts to a designer-assisted mode.

Do I have to be home for the install? +

For the start of install, yes — we walk through the layout, confirm the wall, and unbox. After that, you can leave. Most installs finish in 6–8 hours and we send a video walkthrough on completion.

How does the 10-year warranty work? +

Panels, hardware and installation are all covered. If anything fails — a hinge, a soft-close, a panel warp — we replace the part and re-install free. The warranty is registered to your address, not the project, so it survives rentals or resale.

Where does Kreobox operate today? +

Direct channel is live in Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad. NCR launches Q3 2026. Tier-2 cities follow through the certified contractor network — same SKUs, same SLA, same warranty.
