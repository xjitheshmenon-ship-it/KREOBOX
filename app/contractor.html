06 — Questions

Common things
contractors ask.

Do I keep my existing carpentry work? +

Yes. Kreobox certification doesn't restrict your other work. Most certified contractors run a hybrid book — Kreobox SKU installs as the volume layer, custom carpentry for edge cases. Many phase the legacy work out within the first year because the economics shift fast.

What's the difference between Silver, Gold and Platinum? +

Tier reflects volume, install score (rated by the homeowner and Kreobox QA), and modules certified. Silver pays 12%, Gold 18%, Platinum 24% of project value. You move up by completing installs at A-or-above quality.

Do I need to buy inventory upfront? +

No. Inventory is held by Kreobox at the city depot. You pick up the kit on the morning of install, return any defective panels under warranty. Your only kit is your install toolkit — drill, level, soft-close jig.

What if a homeowner is unhappy with an install I did? +

Kreobox handles the customer interface and runs a 30-day post-install audit. Genuine defects are replaced at our cost. Install-quality issues are debriefed with you 1:1 and fed into your install score. Repeat issues affect tier; isolated misses do not.

Can I bring my team? +

Yes. Your team installs under your certification once they pass the assembly assessment (1-day, at the depot). Most certified contractors run a crew of 2–4 installers.

When do payouts happen? +

Weekly UPI transfers, every Friday, for installs handed over and signed-off in the preceding week. Holds only apply if a customer complaint is active.
